- Includes mistakes and growth moments
- Best for career insights and transitions

### 2. **Educational & Practical** (8 posts)

- Examples and use cases
- Best for cheat sheets and technical guides

### 3. **Business-Centric** (9 posts)

- Best for strategic insights

### 4. **Conversational & Approachable** (6 posts)
