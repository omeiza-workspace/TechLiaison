## Engagement Strategy

### Post-Engagement Actions

**Immediate (within 2 hours):**
- Reply to all comments
- Like thoughtful responses
- Share valuable comments

**Within 24 hours:**
- Thank commenters for insights
- Answer questions from comments
- Share posts to relevant groups

**Ongoing:**
- Monitor LinkedIn analytics
- Adjust content based on performance
- Engage with other professionals' posts

### Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Engagement Rate | 10-20% | (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions |
| Comment Quality | High (substantive) | Monitor for meaningful vs generic |
| Share Rate | 15-25% | Shares / Views |
| Connection Growth | 5-10 new/week | Net new connections |
| Profile Visits | 100+ / month | People viewing profile after posts |
